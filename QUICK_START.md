# ⚡ Quick Start - Music Hub

## 1 Minute to Get Started

### 1️⃣ Start Frontend
Open `public/index.html` with Live Server (right-click -> Open with Live Server)

### 2️⃣ Open Music Tab
- Click 🎵 Music
- Choose one of the 4 free options:
	- YouTube Lo-fi
	- SoundCloud Chill
	- 94.7 FM (South Africa)
	- 5FM (South Africa)

### 3️⃣ Play Music
Use the player controls inside the embedded frame.

---

## Notes
- No account login required
- No backend required
- Works with free sources
